# Share Everything

Slay the Spire 2 multiplayer mod with configurable sharing for HP, Block, buffs/debuffs, gold, decks, energy, relics, and potions.

Target game build: `v0.111.0` (2026-08-13).

## Rules

- HP sharing is enabled by default. Shared current and maximum HP are the sums of the hidden per-player contribution ledgers.
- Block sharing is enabled by default. Shared Block is the sum of each player's Block contribution.
- Buffs and debuffs remain personal by default. The host can optionally share player buffs and debuffs with the whole party; each player receives an independent Power instance so target-specific hooks and durations still work.
- Gold remains personal by default. The host can optionally enable one shared balance whose initial value is the sum of every player's gold; any player's gain or payment updates that party total exactly once, and every header displays the same total.
- Decks remain personal by default. When shared, every player receives one identical deck. New runs start with three Strikes, three Defends, and every participant's character-specific starter cards; later additions, removals, upgrades, enchantments, and afflictions are mirrored exactly once. The top-bar count and any open smith/removal card grid continuously reconcile with the current shared deck.
- Energy remains personal by default. When shared, current energy is one party pool and maximum energy is the sum of every player's maximum-energy contribution.
- Relics remain personal by default. When shared, starting relics are combined and later acquisition, removal, or melting is mirrored to every player. Relic acquisition effects resolve once; any relic, potion, or permanent-deck mutations they trigger are then synchronized through their own sharing paths.
- Potions remain personal by default. When shared, potion-slot counts are summed and every slot, acquisition, use, and discard is mirrored to every player, including potions generated by another potion or relic.
- Event choices remain selectable when their shared card, relic, or potion cost was already consumed by another player. Choosing one finishes that player's event as a no-op, without applying its cost or reward.
- Block granted to another player is credited to that recipient.
- Effects referring to “your Block” use only that player's Block contribution.
- Percentage healing uses the shared maximum HP; fixed healing is unchanged.
- Osty's visible HP is shared, while Osty card effects use the owner's contribution. Damage is removed proportionally from all Osty contributions, and only damage beyond the entire shared Osty pool spills into shared player HP.
- While the shared Osty pool is alive, powered attacks against any player are redirected to it, including players who do not personally own an Osty. HP-loss costs and non-powered damage retain vanilla behavior.
- Enemy attack damage is multiplied by the configurable coefficient (default `1.10`). An all-player attack therefore naturally deals roughly `players x 1.10` of its single-player total. Multi-hit intents display the combined per-hit damage across every targeted player as `damage x hits (total)`.
- Multi-target attacks retain their individual target calculations. Floating damage numbers are aggregated separately into one shared-Osty total and one shared-player total after the attack batch resolves.

The contribution labels beside Block and Osty HP have localized Japanese/English hover explanations. The multiplayer roster keeps character/name/hand/energy UI but hides its redundant HP/Block strip.

The supported sharing options and enemy multiplier are available in the settings screen. They cannot be changed during a run, and the host's values are synchronized to all clients. HP and Block default to enabled; the other sharing options default to disabled.

## Build

The project has no package dependencies and references the DLLs already installed with the game. Build with:

```powershell
dotnet restore --configfile NuGet.Config
dotnet build --no-restore -c Release
```

After building, copy the generated `dist/ShareEverything` folder into the game's local mod directory.
